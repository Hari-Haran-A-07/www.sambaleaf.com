# SAMBALEAF Design System & Cultural Visual Identity

## 1. Color Palette & Emotional Resonance
The SAMBALEAF palette draws direct inspiration from the hearth, seasoned copper degas, plantain groves, and sun-dried spices.

| Token | Hex | Role | Cultural Inspiration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `charcoal-pure` | `#0B0A08` | Background & Depth | Tamarind wood charcoal embers |
| `charcoal-deep` | `#141310` | Elevated Surfaces & Cards | Cast iron hearth & weathered dega base |
| `charcoal-near` | `#1D1C19` | Main Section Background | Warm kitchen night atmosphere |
| `gold-brass` | `#C99A3A` | Primary Brand Accent | Heritage polished brass ladles & degas |
| `gold-turmeric` | `#D6B56A` | Hover & Highlights | Freshly stone-ground Erode turmeric |
| `terracotta` | `#A95332` | Urgency & Action Badges | Ramnad red chillies & baked clay pots |
| `leaf-emerald` | `#2D5A38` | Halal & Sustainability | Freshly cut plantain leaf polyphenol sheen |
| `cream` | `#F5F2EB` | Primary Body Text | Fine aged Seeraga Samba starch grain |

## 2. Typography Matrix

- **Display & Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond`
  - High-contrast, editorial serif conveying culinary majesty, heritage, and timeless Tamil gastronomy.
- **Body & Controls**: `Inter`
  - High legibility at small sizes, optimal for ingredients, allergen notes, and price tags.
- **Tamil Native Script**: `Noto Serif Tamil`
  - Culturally faithful rendering of classical Tamil typography (திண்டுக்கல் சீரக சம்பா பிரியாணி).
- **Technical & Numeric**: `JetBrains Mono`
  - Used for Order IDs (`SL-7824`), timestamps, and thermal kitchen receipts.

## 3. Elevation & Border Stylings
- **Card Surfaces**: `bg-charcoal-deep/90 border border-cream/10 rounded-2xl`
- **Active Focus**: `ring-1 ring-gold/40 border-gold shadow-2xl`
- **Subtle Glow**: Gold radial gradient blur on section headers (`bg-gold/5 blur-3xl`)
